# Learning Operations Aggregate

Last verified: 2026-09-23.

This aggregate connects the roster to the calendar. Lifecycle actions must not
silently erase historical finance or scheduling relationships.

## Group

| Concern              | Contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Purpose              | Teaching cohort with optional default price, optional teacher, optional seat count (1–500, informational) and roster.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Ownership            | Workspace-scoped.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Relationships        | Optional teacher (`teacherId`, `SET NULL`), enrollments (each member's packages hang off their enrollment), lessons, series.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Create/update        | Roster reconciliation creates, reactivates or archives enrollments in batches (student-archived rows are left alone); price fallback is group, then student, then free. A school must name a teacher before students or a schedule (`GROUP_TEACHER_REQUIRED`); a solo workspace uses its only teacher. Create/update may add the **first** schedule only (`409 GROUP_SCHEDULE_EXISTS` otherwise), conflict-checked and materialized at once. Changing the teacher moves live enrollments, live/roster-suspended series and future `SCHEDULED` lessons after one batched conflict check; held lessons keep their teacher. Writes are audited. |
| Archive              | Owner-only and idempotent. An archived group stays readable (`GET /groups/:id`). It preserves roster, all finance, audit history, completed/non-scheduled lessons, and every foreign key. It archives group series and only future `SCHEDULED` group lessons.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Restore              | Owner-only and idempotent. It restores only series/lessons marked by the matching group archive timestamp, after checking every restored lesson against live teacher conflicts.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Active/paused roster | The default operational roster/counts include only `ACTIVE`/`PAUSED` enrollments whose student is not archived. Student archive preserves `groupId`, temporarily archives its enrollment, and restore returns its exact preceding `ACTIVE`/`PAUSED` state.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

Acceptance scenarios: archive empty group; archive active/paused group;
preserve completed lessons and finance; restore roster; repeated archive/restore;
cross-workspace and non-owner denial.

A group recurring series materializes only while it has at least one active,
non-archived participant. The roster-empty transition token-suspends future
scheduled work; the first active participant restores only that marked work
after checking teacher conflicts.

## LessonAttendance

| Concern       | Contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Purpose       | One per-student mark for one lesson: `PRESENT`, `ABSENT` or `EXCUSED`, with `markedAt` and `markedById` ([ADR 0006](../decisions/0006-group-teacher-capacity-and-attendance.md)).                                                                                                                     |
| Ownership     | Workspace-scoped; unique per (lesson, enrollment).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Relationships | Lesson and enrollment, both `RESTRICT`: a marked lesson is never hard-deleted, and a series regeneration keeps a started lesson that has marks.                                                                                                                                                       |
| Write         | `PUT /lessons/:id/attendance` upserts marks for participants: the live group roster, anyone already marked, or the individual lesson's enrollment. The lesson must have started and not be cancelled (`409 ATTENDANCE_NOT_MARKABLE`). Audited on the lesson.                                          |
| Read          | `GET /groups/:id/attendance` summarizes the current roster over the last N held lessons with `summarizeAttendance` from `@tutorio/domain`: cancelled lessons count for nobody, excused is neutral, on-hold participants stay out of group figures, risk is a trailing streak of two or more absences. |
| Held lesson   | When a group lesson becomes held — by the job, by hand, or created held — every active member without a mark is marked `PRESENT` (no author, L-72); the tutor changes only the exceptions. Marks decide who is charged: excused is free ([finance](./finance.md#lessoncharge)).                       |
| Current gap   | Marking lives in a dialog on the group page until the lesson screen owns it (Work Packet 6.4 screens).                                                                                                                                                                                                |

## Enrollment

| Concern               | Contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Purpose               | Authoritative student-to-individual/group teaching relationship and billing-policy snapshot.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Ownership             | Workspace-scoped.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Relationships         | Student, teacher, optional group; lessons, series, packages, lesson charges and payments.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Create/update         | Relationship identity is immutable after create; status, billing mode (`billingType`: `PER_LESSON` by default, `PACKAGE` after the first sale), price (the student's rate for this direction), and deadline are editable. A mode change affects only later charges. Partial unique indexes prevent equivalent live duplicates.                                                                                                                                         |
| Lifecycle             | `ACTIVE`, `PAUSED`, `ARCHIVED`; technical soft delete/restore is separate and restore rechecks uniqueness. Update and restore take the student lifecycle lock first; while the student is `ARCHIVED`, a move to `ACTIVE`/`PAUSED` and any restore return `409 STUDENT_ARCHIVED_REQUIRES_RESTORE`. Restore also requires a live group (`GROUP_NOT_FOUND`) and teacher (`TEACHER_NOT_FOUND`). A manual status change or a restore clears a stale student-archive marker. |
| Required side effects | Pause/archive/delete token-suspends only future `SCHEDULED` individual work. Resume/restore rechecks conflicts and restores only rows marked by that action.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Current gap           | No known Work Packet 4 lifecycle gap.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

Acceptance scenarios: individual/group uniqueness, pause/resume, future-lesson
treatment, duplicate-safe restore, and package/payment history preservation.

## Schedule

| Concern       | Contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Purpose       | The recurring plan of one direction ([`product/scheduling.md`](../product/scheduling.md) L-20…L-27): weekdays each with a start time, one duration, a timezone, a horizon in weeks and an optional end.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Ownership     | Workspace-scoped.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Relationships | Exactly one of an individual enrollment (a student with one teacher) or a group (taught by its teacher); its versioned `LessonSeries` rows.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Create        | `POST /schedules` (or the group form, the package form, the pattern screen). Checks teacher and student conflicts of the lessons it would generate unless `force`; a second active schedule for the direction is `409 SCHEDULE_EXISTS` (partial unique indexes). The horizon defaults to `Workspace.scheduleHorizonWeeks` (4).                                                                  |
| Change        | From a date (default now, never earlier): the rows in force end there and new rows start there. Scheduled lessons from that date move 1:1 to the new times (same week, same weekday first), keeping id, topic, notes and history; only the rest are removed softly or created. Held, cancelled, detached and marked lessons are never touched. The preview returns exactly what the apply does. |
| Stop          | From a date: scheduled lessons from then on are removed softly, the others stay; the schedule becomes `ENDED` (at once, or when a future stop date passes) and the direction can get a new one.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Horizon       | `PATCH /schedules/:id` changes `horizonWeeks`; a longer horizon generates the extra weeks at once. A nightly job tops every active schedule up to its horizon and ends schedules whose end date has passed.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Suspension    | A paused student or an empty group suspends the rows with a token; a change keeps the token on the new rows so the return revives the current rule.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

Acceptance scenarios: preview equals apply, topic survives a time change, add a
day, this-and-following keeps other weekdays, stop keeps hand-moved lessons,
second schedule refused, student conflict on create, horizon growth.

## LessonSeries

| Concern        | Contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Purpose        | One start time of one version of a schedule: the weekdays sharing that local time, from `startDate` to an optional `endsAt`, materializing UTC lesson occurrences.                                                                                                                       |
| Ownership      | Workspace-scoped.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Relationships  | Its schedule; the schedule's enrollment or group and teacher; optional package; generated lessons.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Create/update  | Written by the schedule service. The legacy `/lesson-series` routes stay for the current screens: create adds days to the direction's schedule (or creates one with the former 12-week horizon), update and delete change the schedule from now on, and deleting the last days stops it. |
| Delete/restore | A version replaced before it started is soft-deleted; an ended version keeps its rows for history. Restore is not implemented.                                                                                                                                                           |

Acceptance scenarios: DST boundaries, overlapping series, weekday shift,
detached occurrence preservation, pause/archive propagation, delete idempotency.

## Lesson

| Concern       | Contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Purpose       | One scheduled occurrence with UTC time, teacher, target, price/currency snapshot, status, kind (`REGULAR` or `MAKEUP`), optional topic, and one charge per participant once it is charged ([finance](./finance.md#lessoncharge)).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Ownership     | Workspace-scoped.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Relationships | Enrollment/group, teacher, optional series, lesson charges, attendance, and for a makeup the original lesson (`originalLessonId`, one makeup per lesson).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Create/update | Create, move and edit check teacher **and student** conflicts (the student's individual lessons and the lessons of groups they actively attend) unless `force`; a 409 lists each overlap with its reason. `PATCH` changes topic, notes, duration, teacher (a substitution; a series lesson becomes detached), price and `paidAt`. Price/currency become immutable after a non-zero credit entry. A lesson may be created in the past, held by default.                                         |
| Status        | `SCHEDULED` or a final status: `COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED_CHARGED`, `CANCELLED_UNCHARGED`, `NO_SHOW` (individual only). Any final status can be corrected to another; only a change in whether it is charged moves a credit. Back to `SCHEDULED` only before the lesson ends (`409 LESSON_ENDED`).                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Charges       | Re-evaluated after every status and attendance change: held, charged-cancelled and no-show lessons are charged per participant (excused group members are not), from the oldest valid package, on debt, or on the pay-per-lesson balance. The price can change until payments reach the lesson (`409 LESSON_PAID`).                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Delete        | A lesson with an active charge cannot be deleted (`409 LESSON_CHARGED`) until it is cancelled free or returned to `SCHEDULED`. An uncharged lesson archives/detaches idempotently.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Makeup        | `POST /lessons/:id/makeup` for a cancelled or no-show individual lesson (`409 MAKEUP_NOT_ALLOWED` / `MAKEUP_EXISTS`). Exactly one of the pair is charged; correcting the original re-evaluates the makeup.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Automation    | A job every 10 minutes holds each scheduled lesson whose end has passed (`completedAt` = its end, charged, audited without an actor) with the same conditional update a manual status change uses. Bulk cancel (`POST /lessons/bulk-cancel[/preview]`) makes one teacher's or the studio's scheduled lessons in `[from, to)` free cancellations by the teacher with a reason; held, cancelled and no-show lessons stay.                                                                        |
| Reads         | `GET /lessons` is the calendar feed for a period. `GET /lessons/list` is the Lessons list: paged, newest first, filtered by period, teacher, student (their own lessons and their groups'), group and status, with the quick filters unpaid, cancelled, no-show and needs a makeup (a cancelled or missed individual lesson with no live makeup) and their counts. `GET /lessons/:id` is the side panel: the lesson, its original or makeup, its schedule and its audit history, newest first. |
| Makeup delete | Deleting a makeup unlinks it from its original, which may then get a new one.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Current gaps  | `paidAt` is kept for the current lesson form but billing derives payment from the balance. A paused participant is left out of group lessons (L-73).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

Acceptance scenarios: conflict/force parsing, every state transition, repeated
command idempotency, charged and uncharged cancellation, restore, archive with
credit, filters, DST, and series edit scope.

## Pause

| Concern       | Contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Purpose       | A break for the whole student or one direction ([`product/scheduling.md`](../product/scheduling.md) L-100…L-104): from a start (now or later, never earlier) until an optional exclusive end.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Ownership     | Workspace-scoped; belongs to a student, optionally to one of their enrollments.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Create        | `POST /pauses`. Takes the student lifecycle lock; a pause overlapping another of the same lessons (a whole-student pause overlaps any) is `409 PAUSE_OVERLAP`. The individual `SCHEDULED` lessons of the covered directions in the window are soft-deleted with the pause's `suspensionToken`; the materializer skips the window. A bounded pause extends every package of those directions valid at its start by its length and records it in `PausePackageExtension`. |
| While running | A paused participant is not marked or charged in group lessons (L-73); the pause's lessons stay out. A running whole-student pause puts the student `ON_HOLD`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| End           | `POST /pauses/:id/end[?force]` sets `endedAt` = now (or = start for one that has not begun: cancelled). Its lessons from now on come back, checked for teacher conflicts unless `force` (`409 SCHEDULE_CONFLICT`); each extension becomes the length actually used (an open pause extends on return); the schedules of its directions top up. Ending one already over is `409 PAUSE_ENDED`.                                                                             |
| Status sync   | `PATCH /students/:id` `ON_HOLD` opens an open whole-student pause; `ACTIVE` ends the running one. A job every 10 minutes sets `ON_HOLD`/`ACTIVE` when a scheduled pause begins or a bounded one ends; archived students are left alone, and restore re-derives the status.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Removal       | Never deleted, only ended; a student hard delete (no directions, so nothing was taken out) removes their pauses.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

Acceptance scenarios: a bounded pause removes lessons and extends packages;
early end restores them and takes the extension back; the materializer skips
the window and a cancelled pause fills it back; a paused group member is not
marked or charged; an open pause extends by the length it ran; overlap
refused; on hold through a pause and back; the job syncs a scheduled start.
